## my lore

i was born in 2003 in Rostov-on-Don, Russia.

i started programming at the age of 6 with ActionScript 2.0, in the era of Flash games and animations. during school, I learned Pascal, web basics (HTML & CSS), and then basics of backend development with Python.

in high school, I participated in a couple of commercial projects. fun-fact: I've worked on an automation system for a large harvesting machine factory in Rostov ([link](https://rostselmash.com/media/news/donskie-studenty-razrabotali-sistemu-vizualizatsii-glavnogo-sborochnogo-konveyera-dlya-kompanii-rost/), in Russian).

for another project — Pycon, a platform for checking programming homework — I was invited to test it on a real couple of hundred students of a local university.

some of my school projects are listed on my [old website](https://cdarr.ru) and on my [new website](https://adam.ci/#archive) too.
##
i never went to university. after high school, I joined [Art.Lebedev design studio](https://artlebedev.ru), the largest in Russia, as a backend intern.

during 2.5 years in the studio, I was a developer of [Nikolay Ironov](https://www.artlebedev.com/ironov/2/), an AI-designer (back then AI wasn't mainstream)

then I was a lead developer of [Potokus](https://www.artlebedev.com/potokus/), a technology that turns static media into an endless interactive stream, and [Fokus](https://www.artlebedev.com/fokus/), a next-gen slides maker. these two are now flagship products of the studio.

i also gave lectures on caveats and pitfalls of technical design and implementation of those products. a [recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Kja1k-JUBU) from HolyJS conference in Saint-Petersburg (in Russian).

after the studio, I worked on design automation projects with Dodo Pizza (a large IT company and also a pizza delivery chain operating in a dozen countries), and participated in a bunch of smaller projects

in 2025, i moved to Amsterdam and currently work as a software engineer at [Teero](https://teero.com), moving US dental industry forward with AI-native solutions.
